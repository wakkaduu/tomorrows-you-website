Facility Overview

ONE PLAYER. MULTIPLE TIMELINES.

You are trapped inside an anomalous research complex where the laws of time have shattered. The only way out is to turn your previous attempts into synchronous teammates.

The Temporal Facility

Explore Meridian Temporal Research Facility after The Fracture. Project PALIMPSEST preserves human actions as Echoes, turning previous attempts into the key to moving forward.

Holographic Echo Teammates

Use Create Echo to save your current recording and return to spawn. On the next attempt, your Echo replays those actions alongside you, helping hold plates and unlock routes.

Strict Temporal Lifecycles

Echoes do not remain permanently on their final positions. They exist strictly for the duration of their recorded sequence and expire when completed. Careful coordination and WAIT turns are vital to synchronize timelines.

PHASE 01 — RECORD

Record Your Path

Navigate through the chamber. Every step, direction change, and turn is recorded into a temporal action buffer.

Key Mechanic: WAIT extends a recording within the puzzle’s shared allowance.
STEP 01

RECORD

Every successful action and direction move is recorded turn-by-turn during your attempt.

STEP 02

CREATE AN ECHO

Turn your previous attempt into another version of yourself and return to the chamber spawn point.

STEP 03

SYNCHRONIZE

Your Echoes replay simultaneously while you actively control the current player.

STEP 04

CONTROL TIME

Plan recording lengths within the shared WAIT allowance so your Echoes hold plates when you need them.

STEP 05

ESCAPE

Coordinate your timelines, satisfy all door logic conditions, and guide the real player to the exit.

TOMORROW'S YOU is an indie puzzle adventure built around a simple question: What if your previous attempts could help you succeed?

Through Echo recording, temporal coordination, and increasingly difficult puzzles, the game turns past actions into the key to moving forward.

Alpha notice: TOMORROW’S YOU is currently in public alpha testing. Some bugs and compatibility issues may remain.

Distributed directly through GitHub Releases, not Google Play. Verification: Verified on one Android 15 x86_64 emulator. Physical devices and ARM runtime are not yet verified.

Install on Android

  1. Download the official APK using the button above.
  2. Open the downloaded file on your Android device.
  3. If Android asks for permission, allow installation from that trusted source only.
  4. Confirm the installation.
  5. Launch TOMORROW’S YOU.

This is an early Android alpha distributed directly through GitHub Releases, not Google Play. Do not disable Play Protect or other Android security protections to install it.

Existing debug build? A debug version may have a different signing certificate, so installing the alpha over it may fail. Uninstalling the debug app can erase saved progress. Back up anything you need first.

Known limitations

  • Verified on one Android 15 x86_64 emulator only.
  • Physical devices and ARM runtime are not yet verified.
  • Some graphics configurations may fail.
  • Audio, haptics, and gesture navigation are not fully tested.
  • Other bugs may remain.

Alpha includes

  • Ten campaign levels and five difficulty tiers
  • Echo recording and playback, with limited WAIT actions
  • Logic-based doors and MORROW transmissions
  • Incident Archive and daily hints
  • Offline gameplay
